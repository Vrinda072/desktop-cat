# Desktop Cat

A little white-and-grey cat with pink ears and brown eyes who lives on the Windows desktop. She
sits in the corner of the screen, on top of everything else, and keeps you company while you work.

## What she does

- **Naps.** Most of the time she's curled up asleep in the corner, breathing softly.
- **Does little tricks.** Rest the pointer on her and she wakes up for one: a big stretch, a yawn,
  a curious head tilt, a happy wiggle, sitting up, or a short rest. Then she curls back up.
- **Talks to you.** Click her and she says something sweet in a speech bubble. Every so often she
  says something on her own too (every 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, your choice).
- **Wanders.** Every 5 minutes she gets up, walks to the other side of the screen and settles
  down there.
- **Plays.** Sometimes she finds a crumpled paper ball instead, bats it about, and dashes across
  the screen after it.
- **Reminds you to look after yourself.** Drink some water every hour, stretch every 90 minutes
  (she stretches with you), and have a snack every 4 hours.
- **Keeps your to-do list.** A little yellow sticky note sits beside her and follows her around.
  Add tasks, click them to tick them off, and she celebrates each one; a paw print fills in for
  every task done. A few times a day she asks how a task is going. Finished tasks are cleared each
  morning and unfinished ones carry over.
- **Gets special messages.** Now and then a pink message pops up next to her, just for you. It
  stays until you click it.
- **Sleeps properly.** Turn on Sleep Mode and she snuggles under a black-and-white plaid
  blanket. She stops wandering and only talks when you click her, though special messages still
  come through.

You can drag her anywhere on the screen and she'll remember the spot. Clicks on the empty space
around her go straight through to whatever is behind.

## Install

1. Download **DesktopCat-windows** from the latest run on the **Actions** tab and unzip it.
2. Put `DesktopCat.exe` somewhere permanent and double-click it. If Windows warns about it, click
   **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Right-click the cat (or the paw in the tray) and tick **Start with Windows**.

## Her menu

Right-click her, or click the paw print in the system tray:

- **To-Do Note**: show or hide her note.
- **Say Something**: she says something right away.
- **Play With Paper Ball**: she chases her paper ball across the screen.
- **Sleep Mode**: under the blanket she goes.
- **Quiet Mode**: she only talks when you click her.
- **Water & Stretch Reminders**: turn the reminders on or off.
- **Bring Cat Back**: puts her back in the corner if she gets lost.
- **Size**: Small, Medium, Large or Extra Large.
- **Messages**: how often she talks on her own.
- **Start with Windows**: opens her automatically when you sign in.

## Building

Pushing to `main` builds `DesktopCat.exe` on GitHub. To build on a Windows PC instead, install
Python 3.11+ and run `windows\build.bat`.
